### Sanity check: top-1 is a strict subset-in-content of margin and all

Asserted programmatically over all three `.jsonl` files (keyed by `text`):

- **Sentence-set containment**: every sentence in top1 is present in margin
  and in all, with ONE exception -- `"just then she heard something
  splashing about in the pool a little way off ... it was only a mouse that
  had slipped in like herself ."` -- present in top1 but **absent** from
  both margin and all (both hit `cap-hit` for this sentence when built
  concurrently under contention; top1 was built separately, at less
  contention, and squeaked under the 60s cap). No sentence is present in
  margin/all that is absent from top1 (0 "extra" sentences in either
  direction).
- **`tree[0]` identity**: for every sentence present in both files, `tree[0]`
  in margin and in all is byte-identical (`json.dumps(..., sort_keys=True)`
  equality) to top1's single tree. **0 mismatches** in margin, **0
  mismatches** in all.

Net: top1's 987 records are a strict subset-in-content of margin's and
all's 986 records each, modulo the single cap-hit-timing casualty above
(which is a corpus-population difference caused by build-time contention,
not a grounding/forest-policy difference) -- no other mismatch found.

### Margin: what distinguishes a kept 2nd+ tree from top-1's tree[0]

Of 986 margin records, 205 kept 2+ trees (the rest collapsed to top-1's
single tree -- either no 2nd-best hypothesis existed within the 0.02 margin,
or every close alternative shared top-1's core skeleton, i.e. modifier-
attachment wobble only). Among those 205, comparing tree[0]'s core skeleton
(`_tree_core_skeleton`: clause split + core, non-modifier role/predicate
assignment per clause) to the kept 2nd tree's:

| divergence type | count | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| clause-count differs (tree[1] splits/merges clauses differently) | 85 | 41.5% |
| role-assignment differs, same clause count (a core role's relation/word/grounding-type changes) | 120 | 58.5% |
| both | 0 | 0.0% |
| neither (would be a bug -- margin mode's whole gate is skeleton-inequality) | 0 | 0.0% |

Every kept 2nd tree diverges from tree[0] in exactly one of these two ways
(never both, in this population), confirming `_tree_core_skeleton`'s design
intent: the two dimensions it tracks (clause split, core role assignment)
are the two ways two non-tied, non-wobble-only hypotheses can disagree.

### 5 sample sentences, all three modes side by side

Each line is `[predicate: RELATION=word, RELATION=word, ...]` per clause
(core, non-modifier roles only; matches `_tree_core_skeleton`'s own
granularity).

**1.** `one blank-line-separated block = one passage .`
- top1 (1 tree): `[blank-line-separated: SUBJECT=one, OBJECT=block]`
- margin (1 tree): `[blank-line-separated: SUBJECT=one, OBJECT=block]`
- all (2 trees):
  - t0: `[blank-line-separated: SUBJECT=one, OBJECT=block]`
  - t1: `[blank-line-separated: SUBJECT=one]` (OBJECT role dropped)

**2.** `so she was considering in her own mind ( as well as she could , for the hot day made her feel very sleepy and stupid ) , whether the pleasure of making a daisy-chain would be worth the trouble of getting up and picking the daisies , when suddenly a white rabbit with pink eyes ran close by her .`
- top1 (1 tree): `[was: SUBJECT=she, PLACE=sleepy, OBJECT=considering] [was: SUBJECT=she, PLACE=stupid, OBJECT=considering] [close: SUBJECT=stupid, PLACE=her]`
- margin (1 tree): identical to top1
- all (2 trees):
  - t0: identical to top1 (3 clauses)
  - t1: same first two clauses, **3rd clause (`close: ...`) dropped**

**3.** `there was nothing so very remarkable in that ; nor did alice think it so very much out of the way to hear the rabbit say to itself , 'oh dear !`
- top1 (1 tree): `[was: SUBJECT=nothing, OF=way] [think: SUBJECT=alice, OBJECT=it]`
- margin (1 tree): identical to top1
- all (2 trees):
  - t0: identical to top1 (2 clauses)
  - t1: `[was: SUBJECT=nothing, OF=way]` (2nd clause dropped)

**4.** `in another moment down went alice after it , never once considering how in the world she was to get out again .`
- top1 (1 tree): `[was: SUBJECT=she, PLACE=moment, OBJECT=to]`
- margin (1 tree): identical to top1
- all (2 trees):
  - t0: identical to top1
  - t1: `[was: SUBJECT=she, PLACE=moment]` (OBJECT role dropped)

**5.** `the rabbit-hole went straight on like a tunnel for some way , and then dipped suddenly down , so suddenly that alice had not a moment to think about stopping herself before she found herself falling down a very deep well .`
- top1 (1 tree): `[went: SUBJECT=rabbit-hole, PLACE=way] [found: SUBJECT=herself, OBJECT=herself, OBJECT=falling]`
- margin (2 trees):
  - t0: identical to top1 (2 clauses)
  - t1: `[went: SUBJECT=rabbit-hole, PLACE=way]` (2nd clause dropped -- clause-count divergence, within the 0.02 margin, structurally distinct skeleton)
- all (4 trees, core-skeleton view above collapses t0/t2 and t1/t3 --
  they differ only in a MODIFIER role, invisible at this granularity):
  - t0: 2-clause reading, clause 1 carries `COMPLEMENT=straight`
  - t1: 1-clause reading (2nd clause dropped), same `COMPLEMENT=straight`
  - t2: 2-clause reading, clause 1 **without** `COMPLEMENT=straight`
    (a different modifier-attachment for "straight")
  - t3: 1-clause reading without `COMPLEMENT=straight`

Samples 1-4 illustrate the dominant shape of `all` mode's extra trees in
this population: a lower-scoring hypothesis that clause-extracts to a
**strict subset** of top-1's roles/clauses (a role or trailing clause
dropped, never a materially different reading) -- exactly the "spurious
extra hypothesis" shape `margin` mode's skeleton-inequality gate is
designed to filter down (sample 1-4's alternates never clear margin's
"structurally distinct AND within 0.02" bar, or clear it while sample 5's
alternates do for the clause-count axis). Sample 5 also shows the OTHER
thing `all` keeps that `margin` discards: t0/t2 and t1/t3 are pairs that
agree on `_tree_core_skeleton` (same clause split, same core roles) and
differ only in a `MODIFIER_RELATIONS` role (`COMPLEMENT=straight`
attached or not) -- `margin` mode's skeleton-equality check treats each
pair as the same reading (modifier-attachment wobble, not a genuine 2nd
reading) and keeps only one representative per skeleton, so margin's width
here is 2 (one 2-clause tree, one 1-clause tree) where all's is 4 (both
modifier variants of each). This directly illustrates why `all`'s
total-derivation count (3513) is not merely "more genuine structural
ambiguity" but also includes modifier-attachment variants margin mode's
skeleton-based dedup collapses down to one representative.

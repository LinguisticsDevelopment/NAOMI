# Encoder Gold v6 — Candidate Lattice Stats (fixed-extraction teacher)

Generated by `scripts/build_encoder_gold_v2.py` (same v4b pipeline: `--forest
top1` default, POS-aware lemmatized grounding, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`,
`--workers 4`) over `data/corpus/real_gutenberg_*.txt` (the full v4b-16k
Gutenberg population, ALL 9 files kept) PLUS a seeded (seed 0) subset of
125 WHOLE `data/corpus/k12_fairytaleqa_train.txt` stories, **holdout
sentences excluded at the corpus level** (`--exclude-file`, new in this
branch -- see below). Branch: `encoder-gold-v6`, built on top of
`clause-extraction-fix` (B1+C1+B2, `dev/CLAUSE_EXTRACTION_FIX.md`) -- this
is the first full-scale gold built with the FIXED `clause.py`, not the
teacher that silently dropped ~half of all verbs
(`dev/GOLD_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md`).

## What's new in this build (script tweaks, committed)

- `scripts/build_encoder_gold_v2.py`: `load_corpus()` gained `extra_glob_pattern`
  (mix a second corpus population into one build, same first-occurrence-wins
  global dedup across both) and `exclude` (drop already-normalized sentence
  strings, e.g. held-out eval sentences, at the corpus-selection level rather
  than relying on `train_encoder.py --holdout-file` to carve them out of the
  pool post-hoc). Wired up as `--extra-glob`/`--exclude-file`.
- `scripts/build_holdout_gold.py` (new): builds gold for an EXPLICIT sentence
  list (`scripts/make_holdout.py`'s own file format) instead of a corpus
  glob -- reuses `build_encoder_gold_v2`'s own `load_usvs`/`_new_parser`/
  `process_sentences`, so holdout gold is byte-identical in shape to the
  real pipeline's output.
- `scripts/select_k12_whole_stories.py` (new): seeded whole-story subset
  selection over `runs/k12_fairytaleqa_manifest.jsonl`, generalizing the
  81-story pilot `dev/ENCODER_GOLD_V5_K12_STATS.md` hand-picked once into a
  reusable, reproducible script.
- `scripts/cheat_encoder_baseline.py`: pulled over from `origin/k12-bench-fix`
  (not yet merged to mainline) -- the POS-heuristic cheat-baseline floor for
  the encoder's rank-1 edge-F1, used below and in the holdout re-baseline.

## Known pre-existing artifact (not fixed, out of scope)

Every corpus file in this pipeline (all `real_gutenberg_*.txt`, plus
`k12_fairytaleqa_train.txt`) opens with one `#`-prefixed descriptive header
line. `load_corpus()`'s `iter_sentences()` has no comment-stripping logic,
so each header's own English prose gets re-split into 3-4 bogus
pseudo-"sentences" that enter the corpus population like any other
sentence (confirmed directly: `k12_fairytaleqa_train.txt`'s header alone
produces 4). This is the SAME phenomenon
`dev/ENCODER_GOLD_V5_K12_STATS.md`'s "Known artifact" section flagged for a
throwaway subset-extraction file, just never previously traced to the
MAIN committed corpus files themselves -- and it was already present,
unflagged, in `encoder_gold_v4b_16k.jsonl` (9 Gutenberg headers -> ~30-40
junk records out of 10,225, well under 0.5%). Left as-is here: fixing
`load_corpus()`'s sentence splitting to skip comment lines is a corpus-
hygiene fix, not a clause-extraction fix, and changing it would make this
branch's population silently NOT comparable to v4b-16k's. Worth a follow-up
ticket, not fixed in this task.

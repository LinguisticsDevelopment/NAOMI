# NAOMI independent audit — 2026-09-08

## Verdict

The direction is defensible but the plan as written spends the next week on the
half of the system that is already the *least* uncertain, and it bundles three
uncontrolled changes into the one retrain that is supposed to prove it worked.
The single decisive measurement — does the learned encoder do anything on the
sentences the teacher *cannot* parse — has never been taken, and it is cheap.
**CONTINUE WITH CHANGES**: take that measurement (plus two metric-integrity
checks) in the next 48h, before the 16K gold build.

## Findings

**1. The encoder is parser distillation *plus one real thing*, and the real
thing is unmeasured.** Verified at `src/nsm_ct/encoder_model.py:201-236`
(`build_features`): inference needs only tokens, POS and USVS sense candidates
— **no parser call**. So the encoder genuinely runs where the teacher dies
(M62b cap-hits, p90 18.29s on 26+-token sentences, `dev/M62_GOLD_VOLUME_PROBE.md`).
But on teacher-parseable text it is by construction *worse* than the teacher
(edge-F1 0.682 vs teacher = gold). All of its value therefore lives on the
teacher's complement — and there is **zero measurement there**, in 6 days of
ledger. That is the load-bearing gap.

**2. 16 hand-authored records cannot teach four constructions.** Ledger
2026-09-07 "GOLD QA + HAND-GOLD DRAFT": 16 drafts across imperatives /
interjections / elision / synth-args. Against a bulk of ~8-10K teacher records
they are ~0.2% of the data; the encoder will regress to the teacher's
distribution. They *are* gate-validated (linearize+legality+round-trip). Use
them as a **held-out TEST set** — where 16 is enough to measure — not as
training data where 16 is a rounding error.

**3. The "universal / grammar-as-input" property is not implemented.**
`encoder_model.py:133-181` `compute_fired_rules` is **7 hardcoded POS
heuristics** (`N_RULES=7`), and `PRIMES = ["YOU", "<UNK_PRIME>"]` (L70).
`dev/UNIVERSAL_ENCODER_DESIGN.md` §2.2/§6 requires the grammar-rule inventory
to be *retrieved input* so a new language needs no retrain. It isn't. The
Spanish "grammar swap" swapped the tagger and the *test-gold generator*, not
the encoder's grammar channel.

**4. The Spanish evidence is over-sold.** `scripts/build_encoder_gold_es.py`
docstring: the 208 records are generated from `curriculum2.TEMPLATES_ES` over a
6×6×6 name/place/object grid, median 1 tree/sentence, 100% of conjugated verbs
falling back to `entity`. And "sense recall" is grounding-*site* recall — the
team's own mirage finding (2026-09-06). "Cross-lingual transfer proven at
scale" is not what was measured; a template grid was.

**5. Metric asymmetry repeats the mirage, one level up.**
`_best_tree_overlap` (`encoder_model.py:912-925`) selects the **max-overlap
tree out of the emitted forest**, and `beam_decode` returns up to `k=8`. So
edge_precision 0.70 / structure_recall 0.212 are **best-of-8 oracle** numbers;
`overgen_ratio` measures only the *selected* tree's size, so nothing penalises
forest width. Decision 1 (gold → top-1) makes `structure_recall` strictly
easier (one target, eight attempts): v2→v3 numbers will rise for reasons
unrelated to the model. CLAUDE.md's cheat-baseline law applies here and is
currently unenforced.

**6. Candidates-first has quietly been abandoned for structure — and nothing
replaces it.** The 2026-09-04 CORE BOUNDARY decision and Decision 1 are
reconcilable *as data hygiene* (parser hypothesis count ≠ real ambiguity;
token sense candidate sets survive). But the consequence is that structural
ambiguity now has **no supervision anywhere**: gold is single-tree, and
"comprehension resolves attachment" has no training signal and no test. Either
say plainly that the encoder commits to structure, or fund a hand-curated
ambiguity set with a metric. Right now it is asserted, not built.

**7. Evidence quality: the data-limited verdict rests on one point, one seed,
uncontrolled compute.** Ledger 2026-09-07: 0.565 → 0.573 → 0.682 at
n=200/400/788. The 200→400 delta is +0.008 (flat); the whole verdict is the
single n=788 point, one seed, at **fixed 100 epochs** — so n=788 also received
~4× the gradient updates. `tf_acc` 0.814→0.826→0.877 is the more reliable
signal and does support data-limitation, mildly. `dev/ENCODER_DATA_STRATEGY.md`
set this up as a GATE; the gate was passed on the weakest of its two readings.

**8. The memory-frame decoder's own gate already failed, twice, and the plan
still carries it.** `dev/DECODER_MEMORY_DESIGN.md` "Build order" step 1:
*"GATE: if coverage isn't near-total, revisit derivation before building the
model."* Measured 23.1% coverage, connectives 38.6% content and near-unique
(ledger "DECODER-V2 STEP 1", 2026-09-06); richer extraction moved coverage to
30.2% and the content fraction **not at all** (2026-09-06 "RICHER-STRUCTURE
HITS A WALL"). The doc was never revised, and `dev/CURRENT_STATE.md:79-80`
still lists "build the memory-frame decoder" as next action 5. This is the
clearest cut in the plan.

**9. Retiring verbatim round-trip does not break the anti-confab gate, but it
removes the only fidelity check.** The sever-memory ablation is separate and
green (`src/nsm_ct/decoder.py`, 16 tests). Structure-match, though, has a
gold-text control of only ~0.5-0.8 (`CURRENT_STATE.md:22-24`), i.e. 20-50%
noise, and cycle-consistency is gameable by *any* invertible canonical string —
a decoder emitting stilted templates that re-parse correctly scores well while
saying nothing. Keep **confab-rate** (% emitted content tokens not traceable to
a present node — already specced in DECODER_MEMORY_DESIGN "Metrics") as the
hard gate; treat structure-match as a diagnostic until its control clears 0.9.

**10. The churn is normal debugging with one directive failure.** Mirage →
mask → strict_ground → aliasing is a legitimate arc, found by the right move
(audit the harness). The failure was ordering: three fixes were *built and
shipped* before anyone printed an emitted tree. The same shape is about to
repeat — the 16K build bundles top-1 pruning + richer extraction + an 11×
corpus into one retrain with no ablation.

**11. Compute: GPU is not a checkbox, and it is not the bottleneck.**
`scripts/train_encoder.py:167-187` trains **one record at a time** with
gradient accumulation; `encoder_model.py` has no device handling. A GPU port
means vectorising a per-step GRU transition system over padded batches — days
of work, with the win bounded by Python overhead, not FLOPs, at 343K params.
The measured rate (788×100ep = 3.9h → 0.178 s/record-epoch) makes
**16K × 30 epochs ≈ 24h on one CPU box**, and the arms parallelise trivially.

**12. Aurora is not a real deadline here.** `dev/AURORA_SPRINT.md`
"Reprioritization 2026-08-30" already dropped the clock. Nothing in the current
plan is an HPC-shaped job; the doc is stale and should be marked superseded.

## Risks the plan ignores

- **No comprehension-model spec exists.** The anti-confabulation thesis lives
  entirely there; the I/O layer is the supporting half. M53-M57 show binding
  works only under carefully-posed curricula and leaked repeatedly at full
  scale. Every week on the encoder defers the project's actual risk.
- **Teacher-error propagation.** Gold correctness was never audited, only yield
  (2026-09-04 morning report (c)); the later audit found ~4-5% clearly-wrong
  predicate/role nodes. The eval *is* agreement-with-teacher, so the encoder
  can never exceed the teacher's biases; 11× more text is 11× more of them.
- **More data and cleaner data are being asserted together, and the QA says
  they trade off.** New sources parse at 50% (Grimm/Jacobs) with 26-28%
  suspect roles vs Burgess 18% (2026-09-07 QA). Expect ~8-9K records at
  *lower* quality than the current 985.
- **The encoder's clearest independent justification — speed vs the parser —
  has never been measured.** It is a 20-minute measurement.

## Recommendation: CONTINUE WITH CHANGES

Changes: (a) measure the teacher's complement before spending the gold build;
(b) report rank-1 committed-tree metrics and forest width alongside best-of-k;
(c) never bundle — retrain first on an n=788-matched subset of v3 so the old
number stays comparable; (d) cut the memory-frame decoder as specced; (e)
reclassify the 16 hand-gold records as a TEST set.

### 48-hour actions (each with a gate)

1. **Complement probe** (~2h CPU, no training). Sample 100 sentences the
   teacher *fails* (grounding-fail / cap-hit / no-hypothesis buckets from the
   existing 1,475), run the run-2 checkpoint, hand-judge 50 outputs on three
   binary criteria: right predicate, right subject, no phantom nodes.
   **GATE: ≥50% usable → the encoder earns its existence beyond distillation
   and the gold build proceeds. <25% → it is parser distillation; use the
   deterministic parser as the encoder and move the week to comprehension.**
2. **Rank-1 re-score** (<1h, no retrain). Add committed-top-1-tree edge-P/R/F1
   plus mean forest width to `scripts/rescore_encoder.py`; re-score the run-2
   checkpoint on the same n=98 test split. **GATE: rank-1 edge-F1 ≥ 0.55.**
   Below that, the "encoder works" verdict needs the same retraction discipline
   the mirage got.
3. **Seeded scaling replication** (~4h, arms in parallel). Rerun n=200/400/788,
   3 seeds each, at **fixed gradient steps** rather than fixed epochs.
   **GATE: n=788 mean edge-F1 exceeds n=400 mean by >2 pooled SDs → data-limited
   confirmed, 16K build justified.** Otherwise the lever is capacity or
   decoding, and the gold build waits.

Run `Gold_Expand.ipynb` only after 1-3 pass. Decisions 1-6 are cheap and
uncontroversial — implement them in parallel; they are not what is at risk.

### STOP list

- **Stop** building the memory-frame decoder as specced (its own coverage gate
  failed twice; respec to reduced-realization or leave it).
- **Stop** headlining `sense_recall` — it is site recall and it saturates.
- **Stop** citing the Spanish template grid as "cross-lingual transfer at scale."
- **Stop** the GPU-capable-encoder engineering item; 24h of CPU covers 16K×30ep.
- **Stop** hand-authoring hard cases beyond the existing 16 until they have been
  used as a test set and the per-family numbers say what a family costs.
- **Stop** bundling gold changes into one retrain.
- **Stop** consulting `dev/AURORA_SPRINT.md` for sequencing; mark it superseded.

### Where I am uncertain

Whether the encoder generalises past the teacher (action 1 settles it); whether
0.70 survives commitment (action 2); whether the data-limited verdict is real
(action 3). The largest unknown is untouched by all three and by the plan: no
one has yet shown that a small GRU over tensor memory can *resolve* a candidate
lattice at all. A 2-day spike on a toy comprehension task over existing gold
lattices would tell you more about the thesis than the whole 16K build.

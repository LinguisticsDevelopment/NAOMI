# Adversarial review of dev/EXECUTOR_DESIGN.md

Read-only review, 2026-08-30. Against EXECUTOR_DESIGN.md, TRACK_C_DESIGN.md §§1-2/4.5-5, OP_INVENTORY.md §§1/4/5, MIND_INTERFACE.md invariants, CLAUDE.md ops rules, RESEARCH_NOTES M56b/M56c/M57#3, and `src/nsm_ct/clause_reactor.py`/`ltm.py` as they actually stand.

## 1. Where this fails the way NPI/DNC/NMN failed

**(a) The dispatch-table collapse (NMN's weak module specialization) — §1.3 + §2.** `OpSelect` is conditioned on `[state; pool(registers)]`. But `TICK` updates the GRU *once per clause step*, so across the ≤12 op steps the state is a **constant**: the selector has no step index, no previous-op embedding, no program counter that counts. Its only varying input is the register-fill pattern, which is a deterministic function of clause type (question vs statement, which candidate family is populated). The minimum-loss solution is a ~6-way classifier from clause features to one of §2a's six memorized programs. That passes every gate in §3 — parity, no-gold, held-out atoms, forced-wrong (forcing a wrong op breaks *execution*, so it craters regardless of whether routing was learned). §1.3's "program counter" claim is not implemented by §1.3's own loop.

**(b) Soft argument-selection poisons memory keys (the stack-RNN blur, relocated) — §2, D1.** `ArgSelect` picks by softmax at train, argmax at eval, over `Addr`-typed registers. A softmax mixture of `{A_e, A_w, P.addr[0..C-1]}` is a superposed key fed to `entity_memory.query`, whose own contract (TRACK_C §1.1 fact 1) is *exact only for orthonormal keys*. Two consequences: the training-time execution semantics do not exist at eval (a fifth smoke→full inversion, manufactured by construction), and the superposed read is a **cheat channel** — it carries content from every candidate at once, exactly the state-carry shortcut M57#3 measured at 0.500 vs 0.938. Predicted signature: Stage (a) looks excellent, Stage (b) collapses, and it is misread as "TerpreT."

**(c) SCORE cannot be one reducer — §1.2 + §2a.** §1.2 makes `SCORE` "the one learned reducer" generalizing CorefHead/SenseHead/RankHead. But §2a's own programs call it at arity 3, 4, 5 and 6 with different types. One fixed-width MLP cannot consume that; the resolutions are (i) fixed padded slots — then argument routing is decorative, or (ii) per-arity/per-family heads — which is Track A's distinct heads with a router bolted on. Also relevant: M54c *measured* that a single shared scorer plateaus at 0.727-0.737 on senses vs A's 0.863. §1.2 quietly re-runs the losing arm.

**(d) NPI's actual killer — trace authoring — re-enters at Phase 4.** §2a's claim "derived, not authored" is true only for the six families `forward` already executes (Phase 0 extracts traces off its own execution). T1-T3 have no code path, so their "≤10 trace-supervised episodes" are **hand-authored programs**. That is NPI's per-task authoring cost, in the one place the design calls decisive.

**(e) DNC's failure genuinely does not apply.** Candidate sets are perception-enumerated and closed; there is no learned addressing over open memory. Credit that; it is the design's real edge.

## 2. Is the compositional-transfer gate honest? No — four defects.

**Defect 1 — "cheat" has been silently redefined.** In every M53-M57 battery, and in CLAUDE.md's ops rule ("every capability curriculum ships with its cheat-baseline at floor"), *cheat* is a disabled-mechanism probe sitting at floor. §3 redefines it as "Track A's hardwired pipeline, run as-is — the tournament floor, not a disabled-mechanism probe." So T1-T3 ship with **no floor arm at all**, and the KILL clause "no transfer task clears cheat+0.1" references a quantity that is undefined for tasks §4 says Track A "cannot do at all."

**Defect 2 — the bar is arithmetically unreachable, so it can produce a false KILL.** T1 and T3 both compose `QUERY_ENTITY`, measured at 0.677-0.710 and explicitly "not saturated" (M57#3), and T3 also rides `CAND_FOR`, which OP_INVENTORY §5 records as *offline-tested, not the live batch-build path, no tie-break*. A two-hop composition of a 0.70 op with a 0.90 op tops out near 0.63 under perfect routing. GO demands ≥0.8. The gate would fail the executor for defects in ops it did not cause.

**Defect 3 — an undecidable middle.** GO is "≥0.8 on ≥2 of 3 with ≤10 traces"; KILL is "no task clears cheat+0.1 after 20." One task at 0.85 with 15 traces is neither. Collapse to one table.

**Defect 4 — the tasks were reverse-engineered from the ops.** §3 writes T1-T3 *as op sequences*. TRACK_C §4.5 names this exact move ("benchmark-tuned op sets") as a workaround. It also collides with CLAUDE.md's curriculum-design law: the gold-determinant must not be answer-predictive.

**Yes, it can be passed by memorization**, two ways: the §1(a) dispatch table passes every listed arm; and few-shot imitation of a human-written T1-T3 program measures the human's composition.

**Ablations that would catch it** (all cheap, all on data that already exists):
- **Leave-one-family-out (held-out PROGRAM)** — the program-level analogue of M56b's held-out atoms, and the single most valuable addition to this design. Withhold family 3 (write-back redirect) from trace supervision entirely; can the executor emit it from families 2+4's parts? This runs on existing curricula, needs no new data, and gates Phase 4.
- **Register/slot permutation invariance** — at eval, permute per-candidate slot order and rename scratch registers. A composer is invariant; a positional memorizer ("step 4 = EMIT from P.mem") craters.
- **Shuffled-selector arm** — keep the execute layer and SCORE weights, replace trained `OpSelect` with a uniform sampler over type-legal ops. If accuracy holds, routing is decorative. This is the arm §3 is missing: its forced-WRONG-trace crater proves the *ops* are load-bearing, not the *selector*.
- **Hand-written-program oracle on T1-T3** — hardwire the composed program (20 lines, no learning). It measures the op library's composability separately from the executor's search. Report executor-vs-oracle, not executor-vs-0.8.
- **Held-out atoms specifically on ArgSelect** — see §5.
- **Blind curriculum authoring** — T1-T3 written by an agent not shown OP_INVENTORY.

## 3. Is the hardwired pipeline as bootstrap anchor sound?

**As a Phase-1 unit test: excellent** — forced trace, no learning, byte-identity against Track A is a zero-ambiguity check that `execute()` is faithful. Keep it exactly as written.

**As a Phase-2 training anchor: it bakes the pipeline in.** The target distribution has exactly one program per (clause-type, candidate-family) pair, zero variance, and the *intermediate register-value loss* — the thing §2a calls "what makes it a genuine trace" — explicitly punishes any register contents that differ from Track A's hand-execution. So Stage (a) forbids departure; Stage (b)/(c) merely stop rewarding it, while the §3 parity gates reward staying identical. **No phase in this plan ever rewards departing.** The honest reading of a GO under this plan is "Track A re-implemented as a learned interpreter, at up to 12× the per-clause compute."

**What would show departure honestly** (add as measured columns, not prose):
1. `H(program | clause-type features)` on the eval set. If ≈0, it is a dispatch table by measurement.
2. **Departure rate**: fraction of episodes whose emitted op sequence differs from Track A's yet answers correctly. If ~0 across the whole eval set, the legibility claim is vacuous.
3. **Shortcut discovery**: does it ever drop a provably dead op (e.g. `QUERY` on a question step whose `V_read` is unused) more often than a random type-legal walk?
4. Leave-one-family-out (§2). Only this one distinguishes composition from interpolation.

## 4. D1-D5 — my recommended answers

**D1 (soft vs hard selection). Recommend: split by TYPE, not by stage — hard (straight-through) from step one for any slot whose value becomes a memory KEY (`Addr`), soft permitted only where mixture is already the semantics (`Dist`/`Scalar`, i.e. today's `SELECT`/`EMIT`); and report a soft-vs-argmax delta at *every* Phase-2 gate rather than discovering it at Stage (b).** For: a soft `Addr` mixture is an interference-contaminated bilinear key (TRACK_C §1.1 fact 1) — it trains an execution semantics that does not exist at eval and opens the M57 superposition cheat channel; and trace supervision is cross-entropy on the *selector distribution*, which needs no soft execution at all. Against: straight-through on discreteness is precisely the TerpreT dynamic this design calls its sharpest kill signal, and hard `Addr` selection early may under-explore; the mitigation (soft op-choice, hard key-binding) is a hybrid nobody has run here before.

**D2 (second recurrent state). Recommend: NO second GRU — but fix the program counter.** Add a non-recurrent step index + previous-op embedding + type-mask as `OpSelect` inputs. For: a second recurrent state doubles the recurrent surface, reopens the M53 entanglement question M54c retired, and — decisively — a recurrent state spanning op steps can carry the answer *within* a clause, which is the M57 state-carry cheat in a place no existing crater can see. Against: as written, §1.3's selector genuinely cannot tell step 3 from step 9 (the GRU updates once per clause), so "the GRU is the program counter" is false today and something must fill the gap.

**D3 (register file locked?). Recommend: locked — but lock at the END of Phase 1, not now, and publish the frozen table.** For: §3's transfer result is unfalsifiable otherwise, per TRACK_C §4.5. Against: **§1.1's table is already wrong.** It lists 6 per-candidate registers (`addr/feat/prior/mem/tmp/score`); §1.4 and §2a use `P.score_extra`, `P.query_addr`, `P.query_r`, `P.addr_stm`, `P.addr_ltm`, `P.from_ltm` — at least 11 — and §2a program 4's `CAND_FOR(kind=doctor)` has no register anywhere holding the description key. Locking a table that its own gold programs contradict forces either a dishonest workaround or a false KILL. So: write out all six families *and* T1-T3 on paper first (the §1.10 acid test, which already earned its keep once), type-check, then freeze; any post-freeze register is a KILL-adjacent finding, ledgered.

**D4 (trace module). Recommend: new module, and quarantine `mind/ops.py` in the same change.** For: granularity/type mismatch, and coupling a live gate to dead M1-M4 prior art inherits a second unresolved design. Against: two instruction sets in one repo is the seam OP_INVENTORY exists to prevent — so `mind/ops.py` gets a header (or an OP_INVENTORY row) saying it is not the mind's instruction set. Low stakes either way.

**D5 (transfer gate before or after M58). Recommend: neither as posed — split it.** Run the *paper* type-check and the leave-one-family-out test (existing data, no curricula) **now**, before LTM/M58; defer the T1-T3 curriculum gate until after M59b, because T2 rides `link`/`consolidate` that are built-unproven (`src/nsm_ct/ltm.py` exists — M59a — though §1.2 still calls LTM ops `planned`, one milestone stale) and T1/T3 ride `CAND_FOR`, which has never run in a training loop. For: it buys the honest composition signal weeks earlier at ~zero curriculum cost and removes the false-KILL risk of §2 defect 2. Against: it delays the A-vs-C verdict and concedes M58 gets built on Track A — acceptable, since Track A ships as fallback regardless and prose does not test composition.

**The one the lead must not delegate: D1.** D2-D4 are reversible in a day; D5 costs calendar. D1 sets the execution semantics every number is produced under, and a wrong answer there yields results that look good at every gate until Stage (b) — i.e. it manufactures the project's fifth smoke→full inversion, the exact failure mode CLAUDE.md's ops rules exist to prevent.

## 5. Add / delete before code

**Quiet invariant violations — delete these:**
- **`PROVENANCE` must leave the learned op vocabulary.** §2a's programs 1 and 2 `WRITE` with no `PROVENANCE`; program 3 has it. If it is selectable, the executor can *learn to skip the audit log* — violating MIND_INTERFACE invariant #4 and OP_INVENTORY §4 ("no gated write skips the audit trail"). Make it an unconditional side effect of `WRITE`.
- **`FORCE` must leave the op list (§1.2 Control).** OP_INVENTORY marks it test-only, "never reachable from an inference call path." Putting it in the vocabulary makes reachability a masking bug away.
- **Drop "GATE/OVERWRITE/NEGATE reframed as `SCORE` ops."** They are three distinct heads with distinct input shapes (`write_gate: Linear(h,1)`, `decide_truth: Linear(h+d,1)`); folding them into `SCORE` makes §1.4's byte-identity gate impossible and stretches invariant #5's *one resolver contract* into a general-purpose reducer.
- **Drop "no learned halting probability" (§1.3)** while `HALT` is a selectable op ending every gold trace. Halting *is* learned here, as an op choice; say so and keep the hard `K_max` cutoff as the safety net.
- **Stop calling `K_max` the `patience` dial.** Invariant #6 wants a runtime scalar tunable without retraining; a `K_max` baked into the training graph is a hyperparameter wearing the dial's name. MIND_INTERFACE permits fixed-hyperparameter v1 — just don't claim the dial is delivered.

**K_max=12 and the budget invariants.** Parameters are fine: `OpSelect`/`ArgSelect` are small, no inference-time weight growth, sub-MB policy intact (invariants #1/#8 hold on params). The threat is **footprint and wall-clock**, and the design states neither. M57#3 already peaks at **6.4GB at T≈50** ("not finished", AURORA priority 1) with `B×d³` autograd across `T`; `ltm.py` needed `LTM_DETACH` precisely to bound autograd. If any memory-mutating op (`WRITE`/`ATTR_WRITE`/`PROMOTE`) can fire more than once per clause step, the retained tensor-version count multiplies by up to 12 and the gate is simply unrunnable on the machine. **Add an explicit constraint — at most one memory-mutating op per clause step (today's pipeline guarantees this; the executor must too) — and a Phase-1 measured footprint/step-time gate.** Add a compute KILL: if per-epoch time exceeds ~2× Track A, the six-milestone plan is unaffordable under CLAUDE.md's one-run-at-a-time rule. There is currently no compute kill criterion at all.

**Does the register file smuggle knowledge into weights?** The registers themselves do not — they hold no parameters. The back door is one level up: `ArgSelect` scores raw candidate *identity* vectors (`P.addr[i]`, seed-fixed and non-trainable), and `pool(registers)` feeds the same vectors into `OpSelect`. That is precisely the M56b surface — a head learning "attend to the slot holding the `mary` atom" is a name→slot lookup table in weights, invisible until held-out atoms are run. **Fix in design, not in testing: `ArgSelect` over per-candidate slots must consume the per-candidate FEATURE register (`cand_features`, M56b's fix) plus type/role, and must be forbidden raw candidate atoms.** Then keep held-out atoms as the check, per phase, on the selector specifically.

**Also add:** the four §2 ablations (leave-one-family-out is the priority); a real floor arm for T1-T3; the §3 departure metrics; a transfer bar stated relative to the hand-written-program oracle rather than an absolute 0.8; one merged GO/KILL decision table. **Also delete:** T2 from the Phase-4 gate until M59b lands.

## 6. Verdict

**Proceed with changes — and do the paper half of the transfer gate first.** The core bet is better-founded than TRACK_C's own verdict allowed: all three prerequisites really are built, M57#3's cheat gap (0.500 vs 0.938 at 8 entities) is genuine evidence that hidden-state shortcuts do not solve this discourse, and the DNC failure mode truly is out of scope because candidate sets are closed and perception-enumerated. But as written this design has no phase that rewards departing from Track A's single program, a selector that cannot see which step it is on, a shared `SCORE` that its own gold programs call at four different arities, a "cheat" baseline redefined from floor to ceiling, and a transfer bar of 0.8 sitting above the arithmetic ceiling of the ops it composes — so its most likely outcome is a GO that means "Track A re-implemented as an interpreter, 12× slower," with the honesty machinery all green. Before any code: fix D1 by type (hard for keys), fix the program counter (D2), write out all six families plus T1-T3 on paper and *then* freeze the register table (D3), take `PROVENANCE` and `FORCE` out of the learned vocabulary, add the one-memory-write- per-clause constraint plus footprint and wall-clock kill criteria, and re-cut the transfer gate against a hand-written-program oracle with a real floor arm. Then run the cheapest decisive experiment in the whole plan — **leave-one-family-out on data that already exists** — and let it, not T1-T3, decide whether Phase 4 is worth building.

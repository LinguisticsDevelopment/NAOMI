    the floor is already cleared, since the point is now genre diversity,

3. GOLD-BUILD SAFETY FIX (found while smoke-testing, applies to the
   overnight full build too)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
scripts/build_encoder_gold_v2.py's build_record() already caught
ParseResourceExceeded but never actually triggered it: it called
parser._parse_topk_one(sentence, k=TOP_K) with no max_hypotheses/
max_seconds, i.e. fully uncapped. That was harmless at ~1k sentences but
is a real hang risk at 16k+ sentences run unattended overnight. Fixed by
passing the SAME CORPUS_MAX_HYPOTHESES=4000 / CORPUS_MAX_PARSE_SECONDS=10.0
dial nsm_ct.corpus.parse_passage already uses, tagging a capped sentence
"cap-hit" instead of hanging. Confirmed this isn't just decorative: in the
30-sentence smoke sample below, one sentence that previously finished (in
the pre-fix run) now legitimately hits the cap and is safely tagged rather
than run unbounded.

Also added (for the overnight Colab run's benefit):
  - GOLD_OUT_JSONL / GOLD_OUT_STATS env-var overrides for the output paths
    (default unchanged: runs/encoder_gold_v2.jsonl / dev/ENCODER_GOLD_V2_STATS.md)
    so colab/Gold_Expand.ipynb can point straight at a Drive-backed path.
  - out_f.flush() + os.fsync() after every record write, so a killed/
    recycled Colab runtime loses at most the one sentence in flight.

  outcomes: {'no-hypothesis': 6, 'ok': 22, 'grounding-fail': 1, 'cap-hit': 1}
  records emitted (valid lattice): 22 / 30
  modifier relations observed: COMPLEMENT, DESCRIPTION, SPECIFICATION

No crashes. Every emitted record has a valid lattice (>=1 tree, per-role
grounding, token_index recovered). Modifier roles (DESCRIPTION/
SPECIFICATION/COMPLEMENT) are present exactly as the richer mainline
clause.py extraction promises -- confirms the "richer extraction" premise
holds on the newly-added genres, not just the original Burgess/Alice/
Potter/Aesop sources it was built against.

--- example 1 (sentence from Grimms' Fairy Tales) ---
text: "a certain king had a beautiful garden , and in the garden stood a
tree which bore golden apples ."

roles: SUBJECT=king (sense-grounded, 10 candidates), PLACE=garden
(sense-grounded), DESCRIPTION=a (modifier role present)

--- example 2 (sentence from Grimms' Fairy Tales) ---

predicate: "were"

  sources that did resolve -- Grimm/Andersen/Jacobs/McGuffey -- were kept).
  The existing Burgess/Alice content already got a full ~2-5x volume boost
  from the nltk budget increase in section 2(a), so this doesn't leave a
  genre gap, just less additional diversity than ideal.
- No general internet host besides github.com/raw.githubusercontent.com
  was reachable from this sandbox (also tested: wikisource.org, archive.org,
  web.archive.org, gutenberg.net.au, standardebooks.org -- all blocked at
  the CONNECT layer). Any future source additions from this kind of
  sandbox will need to go through a GitHub-hosted mirror the same way.
